The Art of the Prompt

To get the best from Gemini, don't just ask a question. Give it a Persona and Context.

The 4-Step Framework

  • 1. Persona: "Act as an expert Primary 6 teacher in Scotland..."
  • 2. Task: "Design a 45-minute lesson plan about renewable energy..."
  • 3. Context: "The class has varying abilities and we have access to 10 iPads..."
  • 4. Format: "Provide the output as a structured table with clear learning intentions."

Try This Prompt:

Copy & Paste
"Act as a secondary school history teacher. I need to explain the causes of World War I to a group of 14-year-olds. Can you give me 5 creative analogies that link the causes of the war to modern-day social situations that students would understand?"

Safety First

When using Gemini Education, your data is protected. Google does not use your conversations to train its public models.

Anonymize Data

Never enter student names, birthdays, or sensitive identification numbers into any AI tool.

Fact Check

AI can "hallucinate." Always verify dates, complex math, or obscure historical facts before using them in class.

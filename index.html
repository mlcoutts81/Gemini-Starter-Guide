The Art of the Prompt

Use these SQA-aligned examples to get higher quality results from Deep Research and Canvas.

BGE Example (S1-S3)

WW1 Trench Experience

Focus: Empathy and Sensory Experience for Scottish Regiments (e.g., Black Watch).

Copy "Act as a historical researcher. Conduct a Deep Research project into the daily life of a Scottish soldier in the trenches of the Western Front during 1915. Your report must include the sensory experience (sounds/smells), kit rations for the Black Watch, and primary snippets of letters sent home. Format as a 'Fact File' for a student diary entry."
Senior Phase (Nat 5 / Higher)

Origins of WW1 Historiography

Focus: Fischer Thesis, Source Analysis, and Industrial Impact on the Scottish Central Belt.

Copy "Conduct an academic Deep Research inquiry into the historiography of the origins of World War I, specifically focusing on the 'Fischer Thesis'. Analyze the debate between 'German Guilt' vs shared responsibility. Investigate the economic impact on the industrial Central Belt of Scotland. Provide a structured essay outline with academic citations."

Subject-Specific Prompts

Literacy & Creative

Prompt: "Act as a playwright. Create a 3-act script outline based on 'The Kelpies' of Falkirk for a P5 drama lesson. Include stage directions."

STEM & Coding

Prompt: "Act as a Python expert. Write a script that calculates the speed of a Red Bull F1 car over 5 laps, explaining each line with comments."

Music & Arts

Prompt: "Describe a 40-second cinematic soundtrack for a story about a dragon under a Highland Munro. Suggest instruments for a mysterious mood."

Safety First

When using Gemini Education via Glow, your data is protected. Conversations are not used to train public models.

Anonymize

Never enter student names or sensitive identifiers into AI tools.

Fact Check

Always verify dates and complex facts produced by AI before classroom use.

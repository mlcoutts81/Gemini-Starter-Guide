What is Google Gemini?

Gemini is Google’s most capable conversational AI. For teachers, it acts as:

  • Creative Partner: Brainstorming & drafting.
  • Research Assistant: Fact-finding & summarizing.
  • Personal Assistant: Managing routine admin.

Core Classroom Applications

Curriculum

Generate lesson outlines, project-based learning ideas, and creative writing prompts.

Admin

Draft newsletters, professional emails to parents, and summarize long documents.

Differentiation

Adjust reading levels for text and create scaffolded supports for students.

The Art of the Prompt

Better prompts lead to better results. Use the 4-part framework:

Role

"Act as a 5th grade teacher..."

Context

"We are starting a unit on ecosystems..."

Task

"Create 5 trivia questions..."

Pro Tip: Talk to Gemini like a person. If you don't like a result, ask for a revision: "Can you make that more concise?"

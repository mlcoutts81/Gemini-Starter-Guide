Mastering the Prompt

High-quality output depends on context. Use a structured approach to ensure Gemini understands your pedagogical intent.

The P.A.R.T.S. Framework

Build better prompts by including these five key elements. Use this checklist for every complex request.

P Persona Give Gemini a professional identity.
"Act as a SQA examiner..."
A Audience Who is the content for?
"For a class of S2 pupils..."
R Role/Task What is the core instruction?
"Draft a 3-part lesson plan..."
T Tone How should it sound?
"Encouraging and accessible..."
S Specifics List constraints/requirements.
"Include CfE benchmarks..."
Copy "(P) Act as an expert Primary Science teacher. (R) Design a 40-minute experiment on water density (A) for a P5 class. (T) Use clear, step-by-step language. (S) Must align with CfE benchmark SCN 2-05a and include household items."
BGE Level (S1-S3)

The Trench Experience

Focus: Sensory immersion for Scottish Regiments (e.g., The Black Watch).

Copy "Act as a historical researcher. Conduct a Deep Research project into the daily life of a Scottish soldier in the trenches of the Western Front during 1915. Your report must include: The Sensory Experience, Equipment & Rations (standard kit for the Black Watch), and Primary Accounts. Output: Format as a 'Fact File' for a student diary entry."
Senior Phase (Higher)

Historiography Inquiry

Focus: The Fischer Thesis and the industrial impact on the Central Belt.

Copy "Conduct an academic Deep Research inquiry into the historiography of WWI origins, specifically the 'Fischer Thesis.' Research the economic impact on the 'Workshop of the World' (Scottish Central Belt). Output: Provide a structured essay outline with 5 academic citations and a counter-argument section."

Privacy First

Data protection is non-negotiable. When using Gemini through Glow, your data stays within the secure enterprise environment.

Anonymize Everything

Never enter student names, Scottish Candidate Numbers (SCN), or sensitive personal details. Use generic descriptors like 'Student A' or 'P7 Group'.

The 'Human in the Loop'

AI can hallucinate. Always fact-check dates, equations, and local policy references before sharing materials with learners or colleagues.

Glow Security

Using your official Glow account ensures that your conversation data is not used to train public AI models. You own your prompts.
